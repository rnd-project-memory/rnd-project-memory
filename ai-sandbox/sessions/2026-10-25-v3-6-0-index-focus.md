## Reasoning

**The check.** `check.sh` gains *INDEX.md Current focus (limit 15)*. It counts non-blank lines
from the heading to the next `---` or `##`, and leaves out blockquote lines so that guidance
does not count against the bound. Past 15 it prints `?` with where the material belongs. The
warning clears on rewrite (`ADR-013` §4). The adopter copy reports 270 lines, and this
repository's own section reports 39.

**The guidance outlives the blank.** In `skeleton/ai-sandbox/INDEX.md` the *2–4 lines* bound
moves out of the `<<FILL:` marker into a blockquote under the heading, which answering the blank
does not delete. The marker keeps only what to write. One home for the bound, and the check points
at it rather than restating it.

**`checkpoint.md` step 5** said *update* "Current focus", which the adopter read as *add a
paragraph*. It now says *rewrite whole, never add a paragraph for this session*. This fixes a
sentence that admits two readings (`ADR-013` gate 1). It is not a new rule, since the bound was
already shipped, but it is still named in the tag notes, as step 4 requires for playbooks.

**Gates.** `bootstrap-test.sh` gains two: no over-long-focus report on a fresh install, and the
guidance survives answering the blank. It passes. Hashes were regenerated, for `check.sh` and
`playbooks/checkpoint.md`. The handbook's §12 list of checks names both new reports.

**Bump: MINOR.** By §15 the question is whether existing entries still conform. An existing long
*Current focus* already violated the shipped bound, which used to sit in the marker. What is new
is a check that says so, and it expires: it clears on rewrite. The new `INDEX.md` blockquote is
scaffold and reaches new installs only, so existing projects get the check and the playbook
wording, which is enough to act on.

**`README.md` Status** was raised to `v3.6.0`. It also still called the marker-flag removal *the
next measurement* two months after `EXP-2026-08-28-marker-flags` measured it. That sentence was
corrected in passing.
